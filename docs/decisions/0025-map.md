# 0025 — The map (PLAN layer 7; owner's request in `0012`)

Date: 8 Nov 2026. **Decided, not built.** Nothing in this record has been run yet except the
data checks below.

## Human design

The owner wants at least one map, interactive. First idea: the traveler's origin to destination,
with a line for each alternative, colored by whether murphy recommends it, as a map or mixed
map/list view. The professor suggested showing regions or weather on a map.

## AI recommendation and the owner's decision

- **Per-option lines rejected, with evidence:** all 776,125 saved alternatives fly the same route
  as the original (checked on `replay_final_network/pairs.parquet`; `0013` left out nearby airports
  and connections), so every line would be the same line. Spreading them apart would be invented
  geometry. Instead: **one route line colored by the verdict, and the alternatives as a timeline
  beside it** (a bar from departure to arrival, colored recommended or not, clickable).
- AI offered six map ideas; the owner chose one map with layers. **Owner accepted, 8 Nov 2026:**
  1. **Base:** the route, with the alternatives timeline.
  2. **Regions:** airports colored by co-disruption community (`0017`), each region shaded by the
     day's mean forecast risk.
  3. **Linked airports:** co-disruption links from the origin, GCN order (`0022`) highlighting the
     ones likely to go bad next.
  4. **Weather:** observations and TAF as issued, at forecast time.
  5. **Time slider:** animate regions or linked airports hour by hour with the GCN's predicted
     3-hour risk. **Forecasts only**; outcomes stay off the screen (`0016`).
- Not chosen: the inbound-aircraft leg, risk on every route from the origin.
- **Library: d3** with a US outline file from a CDN, pinned (owner approved the dependency).
  Alternative: Leaflet, which needs a third-party tile server.

## Data checks, 8 Nov 2026

- Coordinates (`data/airports.parquet`): 335 of 336 community airports.
- Regions: forecast flights per community-day at 2 h, all 2025: min 143, median 328.
- GCN (`gcn_co_avg.npy`): 26,304 hours x 100 big airports, no blanks.
- Weather (`data/weather/asos.parquet`, `taf.parquet`): **30 airports only**, obs 2022-12-31 to
  2026-08-31.
- Co-disruption links: built in `scripts/build_graph.py` (`summary`, 2023-24) and stored in Neo4j
  as `CO_DISRUPTS`. **Exported 8 Nov 2026** by `scripts/export_links.py` to
  `data/forecast/network/co_disrupts.parquet`: 803 links, 100 airports, 1.3 s from Aura. Checked
  against a fresh `summary()` run: same 803 pairs, weights equal (max difference 4e-16). One row
  per pair, so the links are undirected.

## Limits to state on the map

Weather at 30 airports; links and animation for the 100 big airports (a smaller origin is said
plainly, not drawn empty); the animation is forecasts.
